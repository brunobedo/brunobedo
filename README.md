## 🧑‍🔬 About Me

I hold a B.Sc. in Physical Education and Sport from the School of Physical Education and Sport of Ribeirão Preto, University of São Paulo, and both an M.Sc. (2016) and Ph.D. (2021) from the same institution. I've also worked as a researcher at the **Human Movement Biomechanics Laboratory, University of Ottawa**, and as a **Data Scientist at Grupo Globo** (Organizações Globo Participações S.A.).

I currently serve as a professor in the Department of Sports at the School of Physical Education and Sport, University of São Paulo, and I'm also a professor-member of the **Brazilian Society of Biomechanics**.

My work sits at the intersection of sports science and computation — I use Python, MATLAB, and R for data acquisition and processing in sports performance analysis. My core research interests include:

- 📊 Data Science applied to sports
- 🛰️ Technology applied to Sports
- 🏃 Performance analysis
- 🦴 Biomechanics & Motor Control
- 💻 Computational methods for human movement analysis
